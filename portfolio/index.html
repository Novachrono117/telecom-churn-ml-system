Estimate churn risk for a customer

Estimate the model’s churn score for a customer and inspect the factors contributing to that score. The score is compared against a decision threshold that was selected before the model was frozen — not against a default of 0.5.

This is a production-style inference boundary over a model that is closed: nothing on this page trains, tunes or recalibrates anything, and every number shown comes from the frozen artifacts or from the request you submit.

The 19 contracted features. Values are sent as typed; no customer identifier is accepted and none is stored. The synthetic examples are invented for demonstration and are not rows from any dataset.
